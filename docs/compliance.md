# Evidence checks and professional responsibility

Checked 2026-10-31. These checks help a valuer maintain a workfile. They do not certify a valuation, establish competence or implement every requirement in a valuation standard. The operator supplies source documents and a qualified valuer reviews them. Nothing issues a final valuation or lender certificate.

The [IVSC standards overview](https://ivsc.org/standards/) identifies Scope of Work (IVS 101), Bases of Value (102), Data & Inputs (104), Valuation Models (105), Documentation & Reporting (106) and Real Property Interests (400). The [published edition notice](https://ivsc.org/new-edition-of-the-international-valuation-standards-ivs-published/) identifies the edition effective 31 January 2025. Review the full applicable standard and local professional requirements before use. The public overview is the source of the headings below, not a source for invented numeric thresholds.

| Check | Record checked | Source and exact limit |
|---|---|---|
| SCOPE | Purpose, intended users, basis, valuation date and engagement reference | Supports the topics in IVS 101 and 102. Presence only, not an assessment of adequate instructions. |
| PROPERTY | Area and interest valued | Supports the subject description under IVS 400. Does not verify title or measurement. |
| TITLE | At least one title evidence reference | Local evidence control supporting IVS 104. No title search is performed. |
| INSPECTION | Inspection date and reference | Local workfile control. Some assignments permit a desktop scope; change this control through a reviewed migration for that engagement. |
| CONFLICT | Conflict and competence check reference | Local professional review control supporting the IVS framework. Does not determine independence, registration or competence. |
| COMPARABLES | At least two verified, compatible sales | Local demo policy, not an IVS requirement. Two sales do not establish sufficiency. |
| COMP_REVIEW | Currency, property type, sale date and verification reference | Local input controls supporting IVS 104. Future transactions relative to the valuation date are excluded from the calculation. Verification references are declarations, not independent verification. |
| STALE | Selected sale more than 365 days before valuation | Local demo review threshold, not an IVS rule. Old evidence needs professional judgement and a deliberate change to the policy if acceptable. |
| CONCLUSION | Recorded value, rationale and assumptions | Supports the documentation topic in IVS 106. No opinion is generated automatically. |
| Peer review | Named reviewer differs from valuer, clear checks, current evidence | Local separation control. Names are attribution, not authenticated identities. |

The sales indication is the weighted mean of adjusted sale prices per square metre, multiplied by subject area. Each comparable rate rounds to two decimal places before weighting. Weights are positive relative weights, not required to sum to one. Adjustments range above minus 100 percent through plus 100 percent. Mixed currencies, types, later sales and unverified sales are excluded from the indication and block review when selected. Missing area produces no indication. No currency conversion, income capitalisation, DCF or cost approach is implied.

An authorised valuer must choose the appropriate approach and sources, examine restrictions and assumptions, meet local requirements and approve any final report. NZD and AUD demo currency support does not assert NZ or Australian regulatory compliance. This is a workfile and evidence system, not an automated property valuation service.
